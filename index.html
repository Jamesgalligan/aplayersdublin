04The venues

Two rooms, and three centuries of Dublin between them.

We did not book function space. Saturday sits in a hall the Royal Dublin Society has filled since the 1800s, and Sunday closes in the room where the first Dáil sat.

Saturday 19 December · The main stage

The RDS

Ballsbridge, Dublin 4

Founded in 1731 by fourteen men meeting in rooms at Trinity College, the Royal Dublin Society set out to improve Irish farming, industry, science and the arts. It sat at Leinster House for a century before handing the building to the Irish State in 1923, and has filled its Ballsbridge halls ever since.

Sunday 20 December · The gala

The Round Room

Mansion House, Dawson Street, Dublin 2

Built in about six weeks in 1821 to receive George IV, on a former bowling green beside the Lord Mayor’s house. In January 1919 the first Dáil Éireann sat in this room, and in 1921 the Anglo-Irish Treaty was ratified in it. Black tie, dinner, the awards and live music, here.
