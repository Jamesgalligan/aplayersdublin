Powerscourt Hotel

Powerscourt Estate · Enniskerry, Co. Wicklow

Thirty minutes south of the city, on an estate that began as a 13th-century castle. Richard Cassels rebuilt the house for the first Viscount Powerscourt between 1731 and 1741, and 47 acres of gardens grew up around it. The hotel stands inside the estate, with 200 rooms and a ballroom that holds 600. On Saturday, it is ours.

The Round Room

Mansion House · Dawson Street, Dublin 2

Thrown up in roughly six weeks in 1821 to receive George IV, on a former bowling green beside the Lord Mayor’s house. The architect John Semple took his cue from the Royal Pavilion at Brighton; the city’s aldermen paid for it out of their own pockets because the Corporation was broke. In January 1919 the first Dáil Éireann sat in this room, and in 1921 the Anglo-Irish Treaty was ratified in it.
